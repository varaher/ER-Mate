## The Honest Answer — No, You Don't Need Credit Packs

Here is why:

---

**The credit system was designed for the old app.** When ErMate had a form-based case sheet with separate AI features (Decision Support, ABG Interpretation, Rounds), credits made sense as a gate for each feature.

**The new app is one flow.** Doctor dictates → case note appears. That's it. There's no separate button to tap for AI. The AI is the core product, not an add-on. Gating it with credits breaks the flow at the worst possible moment — mid-shift.

**Credits create confusion and anxiety.** A doctor mid-shift who runs out of credits and can't document a case is a patient safety problem. No doctor will subscribe to a product that might lock them out during a busy shift.

**Pro and Team already solve monetisation.** Unlimited everything on a fixed monthly fee is cleaner, more predictable for the doctor, and more predictable revenue for you.
