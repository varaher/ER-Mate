## The Root Problem

```
ErMate was built database-first.
Data goes INTO structured fields.
Output comes OUT as a PDF or form.

ChatGPT is text-first.
You speak or type.
Text comes back.
You copy it.

Hospitals with EMRs don't need
a database or a PDF.
They need COPYABLE TEXT
in the right format.
Instantly.
```

## What to Tell Your Developer

```
The case sheet tabs — keep them.
They're the data layer.
They fill silently from dictation.

Change ONLY the output presentation:

1. After dictation completes:
   Show the formatted text in the chat
   as a scrollable text block
   NOT as a structured card with boxes

2. One prominent button:
   [Copy all]
   When tapped:
   - Copies plain text to clipboard
   - Shows "Copied ✓"
   - Doctor pastes anywhere

3. Secondary buttons below:
   [View case sheet] — opens 7 tabs
   [Export PDF] — for no-EMR hospitals
   [Discharge summary] — generates it
   [Share] — WhatsApp etc

4. Remove:
   The completion map/percentage UI
   The "Not captured" banner that
   shows HR/BP as missing when they
   are actually present

5. The chat input at the bottom
   handles corrections:
   Doctor types "chest pain not abdominal"
   Text block updates immediately
   Doctor copies the updated version

That's the entire change.
Backend untouched.
Case sheet untouched.
Just the output presentation.
```
