**How to test it:**

1. Tap any case from the list — it opens the case sheet
2. Wait 2 seconds — a **red alert banner** appears at the top: *"New patient arrived — needs attention"*
3. Tap **"Hold & Start New →"** — a confirmation sheet slides up showing exactly what's been saved so far
4. Confirm — goes straight to New Patient form
5. Start the new case — it opens as the active case
6. Look at the **tray at the bottom** — you see all open cases as tabs
7. Tap the held case — it resumes exactly where you left off
8. The **"+"** button in the tray lets you add more cases anytime

**The five components your developer needs to build:**

**1. Red alert banner** — appears when a new patient arrives mid-documentation. One tap holds the current case and opens New Case.

**2. Hold confirmation modal** — shows the doctor exactly what percentage is saved in each tab before they switch. No anxiety about losing work.

**3. Case tray** — permanent bar at the bottom of every case sheet. Shows all open cases as tabs with completion percentage and HELD badge. Tap to switch instantly.

**4. Resume banner** — when a held case is reopened, a yellow banner shows "Resuming — X% complete" with the last note. Doctor knows exactly where they are.

**5. Cases list** — In Progress section at the top showing all held and active cases with completion bars and "Tap to resume" prompt.
