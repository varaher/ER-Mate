The Core Retention Problem in Clinical Apps
Doctors are busy, skeptical, and creatures of habit. If ErMate doesn't save time visibly in the first session, they won't return. So retention starts at onboarding, not after.
Layer 1 — Hook Them Fast (Days 1-7)
Onboarding should feel like a first shift — walk them through one complete patient case as a demo, not a feature tour
Show them the time saved immediately — "This case sheet took 4 minutes. Average paper documentation: 18 minutes." Real numbers, their own session
First voice transcription moment needs to be wow quality — if it stumbles in the first use, they're gone
Layer 2 — Make It Indispensable (Weeks 2-6)
Patient history continuity — if a patient returns, ErMate should surface their previous visit automatically. Doctors love this and it's immediately useful
Shift handover notes — auto-generate a handover summary at end of shift. This solves a real daily pain point no one has fixed well
Personal case log — every doctor builds a log of cases they've seen, searchable by diagnosis. Becomes their personal clinical record over time
The goal here is that ErMate starts holding their data — leaving means losing something valuable
Layer 3 — Build the Habit Loop (Month 2 onwards)
Weekly personal stats — cases documented, time saved, most common presentations. Doctors are competitive and data-driven
The Learn section becomes a retention engine — send one interesting EM case or guideline update per week, relevant to what they've actually been seeing clinically
Streak mechanics in Trivia — subtle, not gamified in a cheap way. "You've answered 3 cases this week" is enough
Layer 4 — Community and Identity (Long Term)
This is your biggest long-term play and most apps miss it entirely.
ErMate EM Network — anonymised case sharing. "An ErMate doctor in Pune managed a case like yours last month — here's what worked." Rare presentations become learning moments across your user base
Leaderboards for the Learn section — residents are naturally competitive, especially around exams
Verified ErMate ER badge for facilities — hospitals that actively use ErMate get a credibility marker. Gives institutions a reason to push adoption internally
Contribute to guidelines — let senior users flag when AI suggestions don't match real outcomes. Makes them feel ownership over the product
The One Metric to Watch
Not DAU, not downloads. Track cases documented per user per week. If that number is growing, everything else follows. If it's flat or falling, no marketing spend will fix it.
